✈️ Cathay baggage — how strict?

Cathay is fully piece-based (they retired the weight-pool system in 2023). It's the number of bags that's capped, each with its own weight limit — not a shared total.

Your fare
Economy Light = 1 checked bag per person, up to 23kg each
7 of you = 7 checked bags total. Each bag also ≤158cm (L+W+H)
Cabin
1 carry-on ≤7kg (56×36×23cm) + 1 personal item each
How strict
The 23kg per-bag cap is enforced at check-in — bags are weighed. Piece count is strict too (a 2nd checked bag = paid excess)
Cabin 7kg is spot-checked when flights are full; usually lenient but don't push a bulging bag
Squeeze plan
Your "2 medium into 2 large" works fine — 1 large case each, kept under 23kg. Weigh at the villa before forwarding (bring a luggage scale)
If over
Excess is ~USD 60–150 per extra/overweight piece — cheaper to redistribute weight between bags at the airport, or ship overflow home by courier
